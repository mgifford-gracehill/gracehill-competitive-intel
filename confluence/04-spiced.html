Top of each column is the AE SPICED deal-review checklist — what you must be able to answer coming out of the call. 'Ask it live' is how to raise it in the room. Don't read the checklist questions aloud; they're written for deal review, not for a prospect.

S  —  Situation

Establish context and buyer persona map.

Ask it live

  • How many properties do you manage, and what are your top priorities this year?

  • What are you tracking today, and how connected is it?

Deal review — what you must be able to answer

  • What's the company, stakeholder, and use case?

  • Who is the main contact and their role?

  • Who do they report to?

  • What are the company's 3 biggest priorities?

  • What are their responsibilities / KPIs?

  • What is their interest in Grace Hill?

P  —  Pain

Uncover urgency and depth of need.

Ask it live

  • When performance dips at a property, how do you decide what to fix first?

Deal review — what you must be able to answer

  • What problems are they solving?

  • Biggest concern?

  • What happens if they do nothing?

  • What have they tried before?

  • Positioning vs. another vendor?

I  —  Impact

Quantify and align to strategic goals.

Ask it live

  • What would it mean if every property performed like your best one?

Deal review — what you must be able to answer

  • Business outcomes targeted?

  • How will success be measured?

  • Why does the business care now?

  • What's the budget?

CE  —  Critical Event

Validate urgency and align motion to timeline.

Ask it live

  • What's happening this year that makes this urgent now rather than next budget cycle?

Deal review — what you must be able to answer

  • When do they need results?

  • What's driving that timeline?

  • Near-term milestones?

  • Cost of waiting three months?

D  —  Decision

Control the process and ensure clarity.

Ask it live

  • Who else needs to be part of this, and what does your approval path look like?

Deal review — what you must be able to answer

  • Who's involved in the decision?

  • Who signs off?

  • Has the champion confirmed readiness?

  • What are the written next steps?

Fit indicators to listen for

  • Relies on individual judgment over a shared standard

  • Inconsistent execution across properties

  • Friction as the portfolio scales

  • Already runs a PMS with adjacent free functionality — so the value case has to clear a real bar

Stage 2 exit — the SQO bar

Impact deeply uncovered

Rational and emotional. In their words, with a number attached where possible.

Critical Event identified

A real deadline or trigger — not just general interest.

Decision process and criteria identified

Who decides, who signs, what the approval path looks like.

Competitors identified

What else is on the shortlist, and which of the four approaches it belongs to.

Stakeholders mapped

Decision Maker engaged or a confirmed route to them.

Technical criteria confirmed

Enough to scope the walkthrough. Custom demo delivered or scheduled.

Mutual Action Plan signed off

Dated next step agreed in writing, with owners on both sides.

All seven have to be true before the opportunity advances to Stage 3. The two most commonly skipped are Critical Event (general interest is not a trigger) and a signed-off Mutual Action Plan with a dated next step. Those are also the two that most often turn into a no-decision later.
