# MESSAGE 1 — The invite

Hi all — I've built a competitive intelligence tool for sales and CS, and it's ready for you to
push on.

**It's at ci-gracehill.com.** Sign in with your Grace Hill email and you'll get a one-time code.

It answers "a competitor just came up, what do I say" in about ten seconds — 98 competitors, one
line each on how to play it, and a downloadable battlecard for the deal you're actually in.

We were losing the same arguments in different rooms, and the answers lived in whoever happened to
remember them. Now they're in one place that updates itself every weekend.

**I started with leaders on purpose** — I didn't want to ask your teams for time before the people
who own the number had a look. That said, **anyone here can add their own team members.** If
someone closer to the calls would get more out of it than you would, bring them in. Just add them
to this channel.

Asks in the next message.

# MESSAGE 2 — The ask

*(pin this one)*

Four things I need, specific rather than general — "looks great" tells me nothing.

**1. Pick a competitor you've lost to.** Read the line at the top of their page. Is that what
you'd actually say on a call? If not, what would you say?

**2. Spend five minutes in Explore.** Five ways in — by outcome, by problem, by persona, by
product, and a capability comparison. Which would you genuinely use? Flag anything that doesn't
make sense or is labeled in language we don't use.

**3. What's missing?** A competitor we don't track, a claim we can't back up, a question you
couldn't answer last week.

**4. What should I cut?** The risk here isn't gaps, it's volume. If a section didn't earn its
space, say so — that's the most useful note you can give me.

**Product team:** please look at the **Product battlecard** specifically — the download dropdown
on any competitor page. It's meant to be the card you'd take into a demo or an RFP. Does the
"where we actually differ" table hold up?

**Account management:** same ask for the **Account management battlecard**. It's written for a
renewal or a cross-sell rather than a new logo. Does it read like the conversation you actually
have?

Post here, or use **Ask / report** in the top bar of any page.

# MESSAGE 3 — What else is running

**Anything wrong can be overridden on the back end.** A correction takes minutes, so don't leave
something alone because it looks baked in. Flag it and the next person on that call gets the fixed
version.

**The weekly digest keeps posting to the two industry channels** — sales-facing in
#industry-competitive_intel, company-wide in #industry-industry_intel. Those stay short, three to
five items. The app has considerably more: everything the weekend sweep found, the full record for
all 98 competitors, and updates that don't clear the bar for a company-wide post. The digest is the
headline; the app is the detail.

**There's a Claude skill for fact-checking content** — uploading it here, called **competitive
content review**. Download it and add it in Claude, or switch it on in our shared skill library if
IT has published it there. Point it at anything that names a competitor and it checks every claim
against this same database, then tells you what to change and why.

**One thing to know:** several companies we compete with are also Grace Hill partners — Yardi,
Entrata, ResMan, Knock, Funnel, ChatMeter, EliseAI. Their records carry a warning at the top
telling you what you can and can't say. Compete on the product, never on the company.

Full detail in the pinned guide. Thanks all.
